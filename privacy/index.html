This Privacy Policy explains what information Foldly (the "App") handles, why, and the choices you have. The App is free, has no user accounts or sign-in, and contains no in-app purchases.

1. Summary

  • You do not create an account and you do not give us your name, email address or phone number inside the App.
  • Your favourites, folding progress, coins, settings and saved creations are stored only on your device.
  • Photos you take with the camera are processed and stored on your device. We do not upload them to our servers.
  • The App shows ads (Google AdMob and mediation partners), which use your device's Advertising ID. The App also uses Firebase for analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration and push notifications, and attribution/measurement providers to measure app installs.
  • Folding guides (images, videos and content data) are downloaded from our content delivery network (CDN).

2. Information we collect and how it is used

We do not sell this information, and we do not use it to identify you personally.

2.2 Information stored only on your device

  • Favourites, folding progress, completed models, coin balance, game state and tutorial flags (stored with Android DataStore).
  • App settings such as language, appearance and reminder time.
  • Your saved creations (photos of your finished folds) in the App's private storage.
  • Downloaded content cache (images and content data).

This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App (see Data Retention).

2.3 Camera and photos

If you choose to photograph your finished origami, the App asks for the Camera permission. The photo is captured and processed on your device and may be saved in the App's private storage. If you choose to share a photo, it is passed to the app you select via the Android share sheet; that sharing is your choice and is subject to the receiving app's policies. If you pick an existing photo from your gallery, the App only reads the item you select. We do not upload camera photos to our servers, and the camera is not used for anything else. You can decline the permission and still use the rest of the App.

2.4 Notifications

The App may show a daily folding reminder that you can turn on or off in Settings. This reminder is scheduled locally on your device. The App may also receive push notifications sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging; to send them, Firebase uses a push token associated with your installation. On Android 13 and later you are asked for the Notifications permission and can refuse or revoke it at any time. The "Receive boot completed" permission is used only to restore your reminder after the device restarts. The "Vibrate" permission is used for light haptic feedback while folding.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Ad personalization: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Opting out does not remove ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Consent: Where required by law (for example the EEA, UK), the App shows a consent form (Google UMP) before ads are loaded. You can change your choice where the App provides this option.
  • Analytics: You can limit data collection by resetting your Advertising ID and by uninstalling the App.

6. Sharing of information

We share information only with the service providers listed in Section 4 so they can provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, push delivery, attribution and content delivery. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data security

Data is sent to Firebase, ad networks and the CDN over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) wherever supported by those services. Data kept on your device is stored in the App's private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Data Retention

  • On your device: Local data stays until you clear the App's storage, delete your creations in the App, or uninstall the App.
  • Third-party SDK data: Analytics, crash and advertising data is retained by the respective providers according to their own retention policies (for example, Firebase Analytics event data is retained for a configurable period, up to 14 months). We do not hold a separate user profile about you.
