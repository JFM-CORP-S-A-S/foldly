Stuck on a fold? Watch it.

Foldly teaches paper folding one step at a time. Pick a model, follow the clear step-by-step diagrams, and when a fold gets tricky tap "Watch this step" to see a short video clip of exactly that step - no scrubbing through a long tutorial. Step clips are available on 50+ models.

WHAT YOU CAN FOLD
• 140+ guides: origami, kirigami (paper cutting) and 3D origami
• Animals, flowers, fruit, dinosaurs, insects, vehicles, toys, snowflakes, boxes and more
• Easy, medium and hard models, with the number of steps and folding time shown up front
• Search, filters and favourites to find your next fold
• Every guide is open from the start - you never have to watch an ad or spend coins to unlock a model

STEP BY STEP, AT YOUR PACE
• One fold per screen, with fold arrows and dashed fold lines
• Jump to any step from the step strip; your progress is saved so you can pick up where you stopped
• Replay a step's video clip in one tap (on models with clips)
• A quick guide to the basic folds, built into the folding screen

MINI FOLD - A PAPER PUZZLE GAME
Swipe or tap to fold a sheet of paper and reveal a hidden picture, level after level. Coins are earned by playing and unlock paper themes inside the game. Coins are virtual and cannot be bought with real money.

SHOW OFF YOUR FOLDS
Finished a model? Take a photo of it with the camera (optional - or pick one from your gallery). Your photos are saved on your device in Creations, where you can browse them and share them with friends.

MORE
• Optional daily folding reminder
• Available in 12 languages: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Vietnamese and Chinese
• No account and no sign-up needed

GOOD TO KNOW
• Free to download. Foldly contains ads.
• Folding guides (images and videos) are provided in the app and loaded from our servers, so an internet connection is needed to load guides and ads.
• The camera and notification permissions are optional and only used for the features described above. Photos you take stay on your device.
• Kirigami involves cutting paper with scissors - please be careful and supervise young children.
