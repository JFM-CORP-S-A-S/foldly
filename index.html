FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in screen at all. Install it and every guide is open.

Does Foldly have ads?

Yes. The app is supported by ads and has no in-app purchases.

Does every model have a video clip?

Every guide has a step-by-step photo for each step. More than 50 models also include a short video clip for the trickier steps; the rest guide you with the step photos.

Do I need an internet connection?

Step photos and video clips are downloaded as you fold, so you need a connection to load them; content you recently opened is kept on your device.

Where do my photos go?

The camera is only used when you choose to photograph a finished model. Photos are saved on your phone and are not uploaded.
